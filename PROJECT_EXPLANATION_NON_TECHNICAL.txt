================================================================================
TOURIST HOTEL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - NON-TECHNICAL EXPLANATION
Titania Hotel, Bandarawela, Sri Lanka
================================================================================

This is a complete hotel website and management system for Titania Hotel. 
It's like having a digital receptionist, booking system, and hotel manager 
all in one place!

================================================================================
CUSTOMER-FACING PAGES (What Guests See)
================================================================================

1. HOME PAGE (Landing Page)
   What it does:
   - This is the first page visitors see when they visit the website
   - Shows beautiful hotel photos that automatically change every 5 seconds
   - Has a welcome message about the hotel
   - Links to other pages like rooms, services, and activities
   - Like a digital front door that welcomes guests to the hotel

2. ACCOMMODATION (Room Booking Page)
   What it does:
   - Shows all available room types with photos and prices
   - Each room card shows:
     * Room name (like "Deluxe Room" or "Presidential Suite")
     * Price per night
     * How many people can stay
     * Room features (WiFi, TV, balcony, etc.)
   - Has a "Book Now" button for each room
   - Opens a booking form where guests can:
     * Choose check-in and check-out dates
     * Enter number of adults and children
     * Fill in their contact details (name, email, phone)
     * Add special requests (like late check-in)
   - After booking, shows a confirmation page with booking reference number
   - Like browsing a catalog and filling out a reservation form

3. TRACK BOOKING (Available on All Pages)
   What it does:
   - Yellow/gold link in the top menu on every page
   - Guests can check their booking status without logging in
   - Just enter the booking reference number (like BK20260110-7236)
   - Shows:
     * Booking status (Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled, Completed)
     * Room type booked
     * Check-in and check-out dates
     * Guest information
     * Confirmation date if confirmed
     * Cancellation reason if cancelled
   - Like a package tracking system but for hotel bookings

4. SERVICES PAGE
   What it does:
   - Lists all hotel services with photos and descriptions
   - Services include:
     * Restaurant (Sri Lankan and international cuisine)
     * Room Service (24/7 in-room dining)
     * Spa & Wellness (massages, treatments)
     * Swimming Pool
     * Laundry Service
     * Airport Shuttle
     * Tour Desk (book local tours)
     * Bar (cocktails and beverages)
     * 24/7 Front Desk
   - Each service has an icon, image, and detailed description
   - Like a hotel brochure showing what's available

5. THINGS TO DO PAGE
   What it does:
   - Shows activities guests can do during their stay
   - Split into two sections:
     
     EXCURSIONS (Outside Activities):
     * Trek to Ella Rock - 4-hour mountain hike
     * Little Adam's Peak - sunrise hike
     * Lipton's Seat - tea plantation views
     * Diyaluma Waterfall - waterfall swimming
     * Nine Arch Bridge - historic railway bridge
     * Tea Factory Tour - see how Ceylon tea is made
     * Ravana Falls - scenic waterfall
     
     HOTEL EXPERIENCES (On-Site Activities):
     * Ayurvedic Spa Treatments
     * Sri Lankan Cooking Classes
     * Cocktail Making Workshop
     * Coconut Arrack Tasting
     * Organic Farm Tour
     * Yoga & Meditation Sessions
     * Cultural Dance Performances
   
   - Each activity shows duration and difficulty level
   - Has "Book Activity" buttons
   - Like a vacation activities menu

6. ABOUT US PAGE
   What it does:
   - Tells the hotel's story and history
   - Shows the hotel's mission and values
   - Introduces the team and staff
   - Explains what makes the hotel special
   - Shows certifications and awards
   - Like reading the hotel's biography

7. CONTACT US PAGE
   What it does:
   - Shows hotel location on a map
   - Lists contact information:
     * Phone number
     * Email address
     * Physical address
   - Has a contact form to send messages
   - Shows social media links
   - Opening hours information
   - Like having all ways to reach the hotel in one place

8. GALLERY PAGE
   What it does:
   - Photo album of the hotel
   - Shows pictures of:
     * Hotel rooms
     * Facilities (pool, restaurant, spa)
     * Hotel exterior and gardens
     * Local attractions
     * Guest experiences
   - Click photos to see them larger
   - Like a digital photo tour of the hotel

9. TESTIMONIALS PAGE
   What it does:
   - Shows reviews from previous guests
   - Each review includes:
     * Guest name
     * Star rating
     * Written review
     * Date of stay
     * Guest photo (if provided)
   - Helps new guests decide if they want to book
   - Like reading TripAdvisor reviews

10. FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) PAGE
    What it does:
    - Answers common questions guests ask
    - Topics include:
      * Check-in/check-out times
      * Cancellation policies
      * Payment methods
      * Pet policies
      * Parking information
      * WiFi availability
      * Breakfast timings
    - Organized by category for easy finding
    - Like having a helpful guide that answers questions 24/7

11. BOOKING CONFIRMATION PAGE
    What it does:
    - Shows after a guest completes a booking
    - Displays:
      * Booking reference number (VERY IMPORTANT - guests need this!)
      * Room details
      * Dates
      * Total cost
      * Guest information
      * Payment instructions
    - Tells guests to save their booking reference
    - Explains how to track their booking
    - Like a receipt you get after shopping

================================================================================
ADMIN-ONLY PAGES (What Hotel Staff See)
================================================================================

12. ADMIN LOGIN PAGE
    What it does:
    - Secure login page for hotel staff
    - Username and password required
    - Only authorized staff can access
    - Like a locked door that only staff have keys for

13. ADMIN DASHBOARD (Control Center)
    What it does:
    - Main control panel for managing the hotel
    - Split into several sections (tabs):

    A. OVERVIEW TAB:
       - Shows important numbers at a glance:
         * Total bookings today
         * Total guests checked in
         * Available rooms
         * Revenue this month
       - Recent bookings list
       - Recent guests list
       - Quick access to important features

    B. BOOKINGS TAB:
       - See all hotel bookings in a table
       - Search bookings by:
         * Guest name
         * Booking reference
         * Date range
         * Status (pending, confirmed, cancelled)
       - Can update booking status
       - Can confirm bookings and assign rooms
       - Can cancel bookings with reason
       - Can see booking details
       - Like a master reservation book

    C. ROOMS TAB:
       - Shows all 30 hotel rooms
       - Organized by room type
       - Each room shows:
         * Room number
         * Floor
         * View type
         * Current status (available, occupied, maintenance)
         * Active bookings
       - Can add new rooms
       - Can change room status
       - Can delete rooms (except occupied ones)
       - Like a room inventory system

    D. ROOM TYPES TAB:
       - Manage room categories
       - 6 room types:
         * Presidential Suite - $350/night
         * Heritage Suite - $280/night
         * Deluxe Double Room - $180/night
         * Premium Garden Wing - $220/night
         * Standard Triple Room - $150/night
         * Standard Double Room - $120/night
       - Can edit prices
       - Can upload new room photos
       - Can change descriptions
       - Like managing a product catalog

    E. REVENUE TAB:
       - See how much money the hotel is making
       - Shows:
         * Daily revenue
         * Monthly revenue
         * Yearly revenue
       - Charts and graphs
       - Revenue by room type
       - Payment status (paid, pending)
       - Like financial reports

    F. GUESTS TAB:
       - List of all guests who have stayed
       - Guest contact information
       - Booking history for each guest
       - Like a guest directory

    G. SETTINGS TAB:
       - Change admin password
       - Change admin username
       - Update profile information
       - System settings
       - Like account settings

================================================================================
HOW IT ALL WORKS TOGETHER
================================================================================

FOR GUESTS:
1. Guest visits website and browses rooms
2. Guest clicks "Book Now" on a room they like
3. Guest fills out booking form with dates and details
4. System creates booking and gives them a booking reference
5. Guest receives confirmation with booking number
6. Guest can check booking status anytime using Track Booking
7. Hotel staff confirms the booking and assigns a room
8. Guest checks in on arrival date

FOR HOTEL STAFF:
1. Staff logs into Admin Dashboard
2. Staff sees new bookings in the Bookings tab
3. Staff reviews booking details
4. Staff confirms booking and assigns a specific room
5. Room status automatically changes to "occupied"
6. Staff can track all bookings, rooms, and revenue
7. When guest checks out, staff changes room status back to "available"
8. If guest cancels, staff can cancel with reason

SPECIAL FEATURES:
- Track Booking works without login (guests can check status anytime)
- Rooms with "occupied" status cannot be deleted (safety feature)
- Automatic booking reference generation (unique ID for each booking)
- Real-time room availability tracking
- Cancellation tracking with reasons and timestamps
- Image upload for room types
- Responsive design (works on phones, tablets, computers)

================================================================================
WHY THIS SYSTEM IS USEFUL
================================================================================

1. 24/7 Booking: Guests can book rooms anytime, even at midnight
2. Reduces Phone Calls: Guests can check booking status themselves
3. Professional Image: Modern website makes hotel look trustworthy
4. Easy Management: Staff can manage everything from one dashboard
5. No Double Bookings: System tracks room availability automatically
6. Better Record Keeping: All bookings and guests are recorded
7. Revenue Tracking: Owners can see financial performance easily
8. Guest Convenience: Easy to book, easy to check status
9. Staff Efficiency: Less manual work, more automation
10. Scalable: Can handle growth as hotel adds more rooms
